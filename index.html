What is Model UN?

Members act as delegates from different countries and work through real world problems the way the United Nations does. You research your country's position, give speeches, negotiate with other delegates, and write resolutions.

Speak up

Build confidence talking in front of a group, at your own pace.

Learn the world

Dig into topics like climate, trade, and human rights.

Work together

Team up with other delegates to write solutions.
